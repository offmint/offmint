# Paper mode, weekend of 26–28 Sep 2026 (FINISH G1)

Read-only mainnet paper mode (`keeper/src/paper*.ts`): at Sat 00:05 UTC it arms a hypothetical one-sided band above the
pool price for every basket token, follows every swap until Mon 00:00 UTC, and settles at Mon 01:00 UTC against the
fresh pool price with the 1% buyback cap. No transactions, no keys. Hypothetical: it assumes our position would not
move the pool.

Two copies ran the same code: the Railway service (the one `/paper` reads, `offmint-keeper-production.up.railway.app`)
and a local run whose files are in `web/public/data/paper/2026-09-26-*.json` (index `web/public/data/paper/index.json`).
Their baskets differ slightly (Railway 87 tokens, local 77); the headline is identical.

## Result: a calm weekend, nothing sold, 0% everywhere

| | Local (77 tokens) | Railway (87 tokens) |
|---|---|---|
| Settled Mon 01:00 UTC | 69 | 69 |
| Armed, never settled | 3 (INDA, PFE, SHOP) | 10 |
| Skipped at arm | 5 (FIX, GEV, INOD, MRNA: pool has no swaps; SPY: last feed update 8 h before window, max 6 h) | 8 |
| Largest premium over the arm price | **RUN +6.19%** (81 swaps), then SKHY +1.85%, JOBY +1.76% | RUN +6.19%, SKHY +1.85%, JOBY +1.76% |
| Tokens at or above the band (+10%) | 0 | 0 |
| Fills / result vs holding | none / **0%** on every token | none / **0%** on every token |

39 of the 72 local tokens with a price never traded above their Saturday arm price. 22 pools had **no swaps at all**
during the window.

**Armed, never settled:** every one of these had zero swaps in the window, so there was never a trade inside the band
(nothing sold) and there is no fresh post-reopen pool trade to settle against. The result is 0% by construction; the
entries simply stay `armed`. Not a loss, not a stuck position (paper mode holds nothing).

## What it shows
This is the normal case the pitch describes: without a supply crunch the vault does nothing and earns about 0%
(the replay: nothing sold on 191 of 198 ticker-weekends, docs/CLAIMS.md). The weekend report's own scan of the same
window agrees: `harm.json` 26 Sep = 58,442 swaps, **0** buys priced more than 5% above the reference.

## Caveats
- Local settlement ran Mon 28 Sep 01:00 UTC at block 74386800.
- Reference = pool price at arm (Sat 00:05 UTC) for tokens without a feed; the paper settle uses the pool price, not
  an oracle.
